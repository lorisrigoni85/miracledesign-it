## What data, why and for how long

| Processing | Data | Legal basis | Retention |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Replying to people who email me | Email address, name and the content of your message | Responding to your request (Art. 6.1.b) | As long as needed to handle the conversation, then up to 24 months |
| Site security (hosting) | IP address and technical data of the visit | Legitimate interest in protecting the site (Art. 6.1.f) | According to GitHub's rules, see below |
| Visit statistics (Google Analytics) | Aggregated browsing data, truncated IP address | Your consent (Art. 6.1.a) | Up to 14 months |

The site has no forms, does not ask you to sign up and collects no other personal data.

## Cookies and tools used

| What | Purpose | When | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browser storage (`md-cookie-consent`) | Remembering whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies | Always: it is technical and needs no consent | 6 months, then you are asked again |
| Google Analytics 4 (cookies `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`) | Counting visits and seeing which pages are read, in aggregate | Only if you accept | Up to 2 years |

Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Limited. Data may also be processed in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. The data is not used for advertising or combined with other Google services.

If you reject, or close the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded and the site works the same way.

## Changing your mind

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time from the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw it, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted. Withdrawal does not affect statistics already collected while consent was active.

## Hosting, domain and email

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. (88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, United States). Like any web server, GitHub Pages logs the IP address of visitors and stores it for security purposes, even if the visitor has no GitHub account. GitHub complies with the EU-U.S. and Swiss-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. More details in [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

The miracledesign.it domain and email are managed by Aruba S.p.A., in Italy.

Fonts are hosted on the site itself: no external font service receives visitor data.

## Who receives the data

Data is never sold or passed on. It is processed only by the providers of the services described above (GitHub, Google, Aruba), each for its own function.

## Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your data, restrict its processing, object to processing based on legitimate interest, or receive it in a readable format (portability). Just write to [loris@miracledesign.it](mailto:loris@miracledesign.it).

If you believe your data is not being handled correctly, you can lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority ([Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it)).
