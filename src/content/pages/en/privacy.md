## Cookies and services used

The site uses only two things.

| What | Purpose | When | Duration |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Browser storage (`md-cookie-consent`) | Remembering whether you accepted or rejected analytics cookies | Always: it is technical and needs no consent | 6 months, then you are asked again |
| Google Analytics 4 (cookies `_ga` and `_ga_<ID>`) | Counting visits and seeing which pages are read, in aggregate | Only if you accept | Up to 2 years |

Google Analytics is provided by Google Ireland Limited. Data may also be processed in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. Google Analytics 4 does not store full IP addresses. The data is not used for advertising or combined with other services.

If you reject, or close the banner, Google Analytics is not loaded and the site works the same way.

## Changing your mind

You can change your choice at any time from the "Cookie preferences" link at the bottom of every page. If you withdraw consent, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted.
